About Paralink

Two disciplines, linked.

Paralink pairs CPA-led finance leadership with the delivery and automation experience to modernize how the work gets done.

Why we exist

Finance is being asked to do two things at once.

Keep the books right and adopt AI. Most companies get help with one or the other.

Accounting firms know what a controlled close looks like, but rarely redesign the work around new tools. Technology firms can build automation, but are not accountable for whether the numbers are right.

Paralink does both, in order. We bring CPA-grade discipline to your books and your close first. Then we introduce automation one workflow at a time, with a CPA reviewing what matters, so the speed you gain never costs you the trust you built.
