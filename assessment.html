Automation Readiness Assessment

Know what to fix before you automate.

A fixed-scope, CPA-led review of your finance function. It answers one question: what needs to be true before you can safely automate and where should you start?

What we review

Five areas that decide whether automation will hold.

Automation is only as good as the books and the process beneath it. We look at each layer.

01

Books and reconciliations

Are balance sheet accounts reconciled and supported? Where are the unexplained balances, stale items, and manual plugs?

02

Close process

How long the close takes, who does what, where it stalls, and which steps depend on one person's memory.

03

Controls and data quality

Approvals, segregation of duties, and the coding and data issues that would cause an automated workflow to produce wrong answers.

04

Systems and integrations

Your general ledger, payroll, billing, expense, and sales platforms: how they are configured, how they connect, and where spreadsheets fill the gaps.

05

Team and roles

Who owns each area, who backs them up, and where your people are spending time on work a machine could draft.

The result

An honest picture and a plan

What is solid, what is at risk, and the order in which to fix and automate, written so a non-accountant can act on it.

What you receive

A roadmap you can act on, with or without us.

The assessment stands on its own. Hand the roadmap to your team, or ask us to help you carry it out.

Deliverables

  • Written findings on your books, reconciliations, and close
  • The control and data-quality gaps that block automation
  • A systems and process inventory
  • A prioritized roadmap: what to fix, then what to automate
  • Where AI and agents fit and where CPA review stays essential
  • A walkthrough session with your leadership team

How it works

Four steps, light on your team's time.

We do the digging. Your team answers questions and gives us read-only access.

01

Kickoff

02

Review

We go through the books, the last few closes, and your reconciliations and map how work actually flows today.

03

04

Roadmap

You receive the written assessment and we walk through it together: findings, priorities, and recommended next steps.

Questions

About the assessment

Who is this for?

Leaders who want to bring AI into finance and need to know whether the foundation is ready and leaders who simply suspect the books or the close are not where they should be and want an independent, expert view.

What if the answer is "we're not ready"?

That is a useful answer and a common one. The roadmap tells you exactly what stands in the way and the order to address it, so the investment in automation pays off when you make it.

Do we have to hire Paralink afterward?

No. The roadmap is yours. Many clients ask us to help carry it out, but the assessment is designed to be useful on its own.

How do you handle our financial data?

We ask for read-only access wherever possible, never request passwords in documents or email, and agree on confidentiality terms before any work begins.
