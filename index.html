Our point of view

You can't automate a mess.

The problem

Growing companies often run on late closes, unreconciled accounts, and spreadsheets only one person understands. AI tools promise to help, but automation layered on unreliable books just produces wrong answers faster.

Our approach

Control first, then automation. We start with CPA-grade fundamentals: a dependable close, clean reconciliations, and reporting you trust. Then we redesign the work so AI can take on the repetitive parts, with a CPA reviewing what matters.

FAQ

Common questions

What is a fractional CFO?

A fractional CFO is a senior finance leader who works with your company part-time. You get the judgment of an experienced CFO for strategy, cash, reporting, and lender or investor conversations without the cost of a full-time executive.

What is the difference between a fractional CFO and a fractional controller?

A controller owns the accuracy of the books: the month-end close, reconciliations, and controls. A CFO uses those numbers to guide decisions about cash, growth, and financing. Paralink provides both and often starts with controller work so the CFO work rests on numbers you can trust.

How does Paralink use AI in accounting?

We use AI for repeatable, well-defined work such as matching transactions, drafting schedules, and flagging unusual items. Anything that affects your financial statements is reviewed by a CPA before it reaches you.

Who do you work with?

Companies under $100M in revenue that need a stronger finance function: founder-led and privately held businesses, usually with a small accounting team or an outside bookkeeper and often at a moment of change such as growth, a financing, or a leadership transition.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. We work remotely with clients anywhere in the United States. On-site visits happen when they are useful, for example at kickoff or during a key close.

Will AI replace our accountant?

No. AI is good at repetitive, well-defined work such as matching transactions and drafting schedules. It is not accountable for your financial statements. We use it to free your people for review and judgment and a CPA signs off on what matters.

Our books are behind. Is it too early to talk?

It is the right time. Catching up and stabilizing the close is where most of our engagements begin and it is the foundation for everything else.

Can you cover for our controller during a leave?

Yes. We step in to run the close, support your team, and keep controls in place, then manage a clean handoff when your controller returns.

Do you replace our CPA firm or tax preparer?

No. We work inside your finance function as your CFO or controller and coordinate with your outside tax and audit firms, so they get clean, timely information.

How do your fees work?

Discovery and the Automation Readiness Assessment are fixed-fee. Ongoing fractional work is a monthly retainer sized to the scope, so you always know the cost in advance.
